# Code of conduct

Treat people here the way a good net control treats a net: patient with new folks, clear, and fair.

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and new hams. Everyone started somewhere.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.

Maintainers can edit or remove comments and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
