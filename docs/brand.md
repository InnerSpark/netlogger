# Net Logger Brand

## Name
- **Net Logger.** Says what it does.
- Heads up: an older Windows program called NetLogger exists. Always write "Net Logger" with a space, and say "for AllStar" in descriptions.

## Look
- Stock shadcn/ui neutral, light and dark. Radio icon (lucide `radio`).
- No logo yet. Keep it plain: the dashboard is a tool.

## Voice
- Plain, short, ham-friendly words. "Check-in", "net control", "link", "key up".
- Say what something does and what to do next. No marketing words.
- Always say it never transmits.
