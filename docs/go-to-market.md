# Net Logger Go-to-Market

## Positioning
- For net control on AllStar-linked nets: a free logger that hears the net and writes down check-ins, so you can run the net instead of typing.
- Free and open source forever (AGPL-3.0). Self-hosted on the AllStar server you already have.

## Pricing
- Free. No paid tier.

## Where to tell people
- AllStarLink community forum (announcements).
- r/amateurradio and r/allstarlink.
- Club newsletters and the nets that use it ("logged by Net Logger" on net reports).
- A short demo video: key up, call shows on the roster.
